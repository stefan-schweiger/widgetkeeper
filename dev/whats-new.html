New widget

Charts

Any value Homey logs in Insights: a line, an area or bars, over 1 hour to 7 days. One chart, two stacked, or two values with a scale each.

New widget

Notes

A short message on a tile, or a handwritten Post-it. Type it in the settings, or let a Flow write it.

New widget

Room Health

The whole house's indoor climate on one card: each room's worst value, and yellow to red the further it's outside the WHO's ranges.

New widget

Value Matrix

Any devices against any values in one table: each room gets a row, each cell green, orange or red against the range you type.

New widget

Days Since

Counters for the chores you do every so often. Each bar fills towards the day it's due; tap a row when it's done.

New widget

Variable Presets

One Logic variable, two to four buttons: a house mode, two setpoints, or Yes and No.

Light Controls

Your room's Moods

Tiles blend every colour their lights shine in. The colour button shows the Homey Moods of the light's room; a tap starts one.

Locks and Doors

An Open button

For locks that can pull the latch, such as Matter locks. Tap it twice to open the door, so a stray tap can't.

Web dashboards

A better settings editor

A widget's settings on a web dashboard were one long list of equal rows: 33 for Flow Buttons. Now they come in cards, with the widget's preview on top.

  • Grouped: each button, tile or column gets a card of its own. They fold, and the header sums up what's set.
  • The right controls: switches, colour swatches, segments for short choices, numbers with their unit.
  • Pickers: devices by room with a search, and values with their readings.
