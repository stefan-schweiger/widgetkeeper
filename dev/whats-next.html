Lights by Room

Every room's lights on a card that switches and dims the whole room. Tap a room's name for its Moods and each light's brightness and colour.

New widget

Web Page

Any web page on the dashboard, drawn at the size it was made for and scaled to fill the widget: no white borders on a phone, nothing cut off on a tablet.

  • Buttons under the page switch between up to four addresses, such as a map's zoom levels.
  • Links never open, and a swipe still scrolls the dashboard, unless you let the page be tapped.
  • {lat} and {lon} in an address become your Homey's location.
  • Reloads every few minutes, for a screen that's always on.
New widget

Keypad

Up to three buttons behind a code: arm the alarm, unlock a door, allow TV time. Each starts a Flow or sets what the widget shows.

  • Codes of 3 to 6 digits, made on the app settings page and never stored as typed. Five wrong ones lock the keypad for a while.
  • Whose code: with Any code, the code's name comes along to your Flows as a tag, and a wrong code can start one too.
  • Show the keypad only when a button needs it, or always.
New widget and Flow cards

Popups

A Flow shows a message for as long as you set, or asks Yes or No, in front of everything: on a web dashboard, or on the phones and tablets with a Popups widget. The first answer from any screen closes it everywhere and goes back to the Flow.

  • Pictures: a camera, refreshed while it's open, a picture's address, or an image tag such as a doorbell's snapshot.
  • Sound: a chime, a doorbell or an alert as it opens, where the browser allows it.
  • Screen names: everywhere, one web dashboard, or the screens whose Popups widget has that name. A third card closes popups early.

Charts and Sparklines

Today and Yesterday

A calendar day as the time span: today from midnight, the line up to now, or all of yesterday with its average.

And also
  • Media: up to eight speaker actions or Flows behind ⋯, up from four.
  • Notes: a long note on as many lines as it needs, with the line breaks you type.
  • Days Since: a Show setting, to list only the counters that are amber and red, or only red. It can be a Smart Stack page too.
  • App settings: tidier, each part a card folded to one line that says where it stands.
  • Lighter on the Homey: less kept in the app's settings, fewer large reads, and Smart Stack pages off screen pause.
  • Spacer: at least 16 px high, as the iPhone app showed a loading indicator over a 0 px one.
Web dashboards

Wide widgets

A widget can span two or more columns: a floor plan, a camera, a chart. Each column it covers keeps its own widgets above and below it. Drag the handle on its right edge in edit mode, or set its Width.

  • Column width: Narrow (a phone's), Normal, Wide, or Fit to window.
  • Sections on the front page: your dashboards under headings of your own.
  • Other apps' widgets sized as on Homey: laid out at a phone's width and scaled to fill the column.
  • Safer: the API key and password are stored more safely, changing the password asks for the current one, and switching devices takes a key that may.
