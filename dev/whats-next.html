New widget

Web Page

Any web page on the dashboard, drawn at the size it was made for and scaled to fill the widget: no white borders on a phone, nothing cut off on a tablet.

  • Buttons under the page switch between up to four addresses, such as a map's zoom levels.
  • Links never open, and a swipe still scrolls the dashboard, unless you let the page be tapped.
  • {lat} and {lon} in an address become your Homey's location.
  • Reloads every few minutes, for a screen that's always on.

Web dashboards

Wide widgets

A widget can span two or more columns: a floor plan, a camera, a chart. Each column it covers keeps its own widgets above and below it.

  • Drag the handle on its right edge in edit mode, or set its Width in its settings.
  • Kept in duplicates and backups.
  • On a phone the page is one column, so a wide widget is as wide as the rest.
