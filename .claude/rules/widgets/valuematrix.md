# Value Matrix widget (`valuematrix`)

- Devices (or their rooms) as rows against capabilities as columns, each cell the value on its level's colour (the user's mock-up, 2026-10-18, a climate table of rooms × °C, %, CO₂, radon; its bottom hint line was left out on purpose, sizes and colours are the design system's). The user chose a generic devices × capabilities matrix over Room Health's fixed measures, and the name Value Matrix. Display only, no taps. `lib/ValueMatrixService.ts`; transparent, Room Health's tile (`#181920`, 10 px radius, rim, 14 px padding). The legend says OK, Warning and Alert (`ok`, `warning`, `alert`), and the range setting is "OK range": the user's choice over the mock-up's Comfortable / Outside / Far outside, which only fit climate values (2026-10-18).
- Settings: `renameTiles`, `colorStyle` (copied from Device Values: the tinted kind), `rows` (`rooms` default, `devices`), `showLegend` (on), then six column groups: `colN` (`Column N: value`, an autocomplete of every number capability on the Homey by id: `listColumns()`, the title from the first device that has it, `units · N devices` as the description, `None` first), `  – OK range` (`rangeN`, text) and `  – Header` (`headerN`, text). The hints are on column 1 only. The `devices` setting has no filter (any number capability can be a column).
- Range text (`valueMatrixRange(text, column)`): `18–24` (`-`, `–`, `—`; `.` or `,` decimals; negatives; swapped when reversed), `<`/`≤`/`<=` a limit, `>`/`≥`/`>=` a limit. Empty: Room Health's defaults for a known capability in its unit only (`KNOWN`: `measure_temperature*` in °C 18–24, `measure_humidity*` in % 30–60, `measure_co2` <1000 (ppm or none), `measure_radon` in Bq/m³ <100, `measure_pm25` <15 and `measure_pm10` <45 (μg/m³ or none), `measure_voc`/`measure_tvoc` (any case) in ppb <1000); `°F` or pCi/L get none. A lone dash or text it can't read: none (the column is `plain`).
- Levels (`valueMatrixLevel()`): `good` inside (edges included), `out` past it, `far` at half the range's width past it or more (one-sided: half the limit, so 1.5 × an upper one; Room Health's temperature margin is the same 3 °C), `plain` without a range, `none` without a reading. `absent` (the row hasn't that capability) is striped (`repeating-linear-gradient` of the track); a missing device's row is `Unavailable` in muted text with `–` cells.
- Rows (`valueMatrixRows(devices, mode)`): rooms as Room Health's (`zone:<id>`, in the order picked; a device without a known zone, or a missing one, is its own row keyed by its id), each value the average of the row's devices with a reading; in `devices` mode one row per device. Rename tiles renames rows by those keys.
- Layout: one grid (`.vm-grid`), the header and the rows `display: contents`, so the name column is `fit-content(40%)` (28 % with more than four columns) and the cells share the rest. Header labels 14 muted centred (the typed header, else `KNOWN`'s label: °C, %, CO₂, Radon, PM2.5, PM10, VOC (Radon and VOC translated), else units of up to 4 characters, else the title). Names 17 medium. Cells 40 px, 8 px radius, the value 17 bold tabular, 14 under 52 px and the 12 px label size under 40 px (container queries); more than four columns drop the thousands separator (`1640`), so six fit on a 300 px card. Numbers to the column's decimals, at most one (up to one without). The legend (12 px swatches, 14 muted labels) only when `showLegend` and a column has a range.
- Colours: tinted (CLAUDE.md *Widget styling*): Muted mixes `--wk-green/orange/red` 22 % into the tile (`--vm-mix`), Vivid fills solid with `--wk-on-*` text. The legend swatches use the same `[data-level]` rules.
- `GET /state?deviceIds=&capabilityIds=` (the picked columns in order, `none` and empty ones skipped by `mount.js`) → `{columns: [{capabilityId, title, units, decimals} | null], devices: [{id, name, zone, values: {[capabilityId]: number | null}} | {id, missing: true}]}`; a capability the device hasn't (or not a readable number) is left out of `values`. Tracked per `<deviceId>:<capabilityId>` (Device Values' pattern), an absent one as an entry without listeners whose signature changes when it appears. Realtime `valuematrix:state {deviceId, capabilityId, value}`. No device picked: `selectDevices`; no column: `selectColumns`.
- `dev/valuematrix-preview.html` (dark, light, Vivid, 300 px with six columns, devices mode, power and battery with a typed header and range, an unavailable device). `npm run test-dashboards -- valuematrix`. A Smart Stack page type. Not yet seen on the Homey or in the phone apps.
