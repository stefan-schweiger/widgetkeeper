# Room Health widget (`roomhealth`)

- The indoor climate per room (wk-058, the user's idea): the picked sensors grouped by their Homey zone, each room a ring with one arc per measure it has, green inside its comfortable range, then yellow to red (`--k`) further out; a line under the rings names the worst problem in the house and what to do (`Bedroom: stuffy, 1,400 ppm. Air it out.`). The user chose (2026-10-16): sensors from the device picker, grouped by room (not room slots, so a plug's or fridge's thermometer stays out unless picked), radon, PM and VOC besides temperature, humidity and CO₂, one range for every room, the worst problem only, and direction B (the segmented ring) of six mocked up in `temp/roomhealth-directions.html` (A one ring per room, C rows, D the house first, E gauges, F large wall rings), one size only. `lib/RoomHealthService.ts`; transparent, Activity Dots' tile.
- Measures (`healthCaps()`, at most one capability per measure, numbers only; from the test Homey's Airthings View, Aqara air monitor and heat pump, 2026-10-16): temperature (`measure_temperature`, without it `.room`/`.indoor`/`.inside`, never `.outdoor`/`.freezer`), humidity (the same), `measure_co2`, `measure_radon` (Bq/m³; pCi/L units × 37, also on realtime), `measure_pm25`, `measure_pm10`, VOC (`measure_voc`/`measure_VOC`/`measure_tvoc`, matched without case, only in ppb). Left out on purpose: CO (Sensor Alarms' job), AQI, PM1/PM0.1, TVOC in μg/m³ or as an index, NOx, string capabilities (`measure_radon_display`) and the `alarm_*` levels.
- The `devices` filter lists the exact ids (the web dashboards' `passesDeviceFilter` matches exactly); whether the phone's picker offers a device with only `measure_temperature.room` (the heat pump) or `measure_VOC` isn't known yet; the fallback is no filter, like Sensor Alarms.
- Settings: `renameTiles`, then the ranges (numbers, units in the titles, the highest temperature and humidity indented under the lowest): `tempMin` 18 (WHO's minimum) and `tempMax` 24 °C, `humidityMin` 30 and `humidityMax` 60 % (40 would call Nordic winters dry), `co2Max` 1000 ppm, `radonMax` 100 Bq/m³ (WHO), `pm25Max` 15 and `pm10Max` 45 μg/m³ (WHO 2021, 24 h), `vocMax` 1000 ppb. `roomHealthRange()` puts in the default for a missing, empty or non-numeric value and swaps a lowest above the highest.
- Score (`roomHealthScore()`): 0 inside the range, else `min(1, distance / margin)`: 3 °C, 10 points, half the limit for the concentrations (red at 1.5 ×). A room's is its worst measure's; the hint is the worst room's (ties: the first room, then the first measure in `MEASURES` order).
- Rooms (`roomHealthRooms()`, front end, after Light Controls' `buildItems()`): by `zone.id` in the order picked (key `zone:<id>`; a sensor without a known zone, or a missing one, is a ring of its own keyed by its id), each measure the average of the room's sensors with a reading. A device with nothing usable is a muted ring (`No readings`), a missing one a dashed ring named `Unavailable`. A failed zones read gives the last zones (`readZones()`), so rooms don't split.
- A ring (68 px, 3 per row at 358 px, `auto-fill` 84 px): one SVG circle per measure (`pathLength` 360, 16° gaps, round caps) in its `data-level` (`good`/`off` with `--k`/`none`); the centre the worst value (17 bold, the unit under it, `°`/`%` attached), else the temperature; the name (14 bold) and the state word (12/16 medium: `Good`, `Stuffy` …, `No readings`). Text colours are the 4.5:1 pairs (Locks' green, the export yellow, Sensor Alarms' red).
- Hint: the template `roomhealth.hint` (`__room__: __problem__, __value__. __action__`; languages may reorder) filled with nodes (`fillTemplate()`): the room bold and the problem in its colour, the room, problem and value each isolated (a `dir` attribute) so Arabic reads right. All fine: a muted `All rooms are comfortable.`; nothing read yet: the line keeps its height, empty. A tap on a ring (`onTap()`, the Activity Dots/Flow Buttons copy) shows its readings there for 6 s (`Bedroom · 18.6°C · 51% · 1,400 ppm`), the ring tinted; not on a missing ring or while renaming.
- `GET /state?deviceIds=` → `{id, name, zone: {id, name} | null, measures: {[measure]: {capabilityId, value}}}` or missing; realtime `roomhealth:state {deviceId, capabilityId, measure, value}` (in the measure's unit). Diagnostics' device list has each device's `health` capabilities. `dev/roomhealth-preview.html` (`?open=zone:bed`). Checked on the Homey through `/web/call` (2026-10-16); not yet seen in a browser or the phone apps, nor on a wall tablet.
