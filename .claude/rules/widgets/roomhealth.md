# Room Health widget (`roomhealth`)

- The indoor climate per room (wk-058, the user's idea): the picked sensors grouped by their Homey zone, each room a cell: its worst value large (else the temperature), its name, and a mark per measure (icons or bars), grey (or green) inside its comfortable range, then yellow to red (`--k`) further out. The user chose (2026-10-16): sensors from the device picker, grouped by room (not room slots, so a plug's or fridge's thermometer stays out unless picked), radon, PM and VOC besides temperature, humidity and CO₂, one range for every room, the worst problem only, and direction B (the segmented ring) of six mocked up in `temp/roomhealth-directions.html`, one size only. **Redesigned 2026-10-18** (unreleased, so no carry-over): the user's two mock-ups (a large value, the name, and a row of bars or of icons) replaced the rings and the hint line (`roomhealth.hint`, `allGood`, `problem*`, `action*` are gone); no legend, sizes from Homey's tokens rather than the mock-ups' pixels. `lib/RoomHealthService.ts`; transparent, Activity Dots' tile.
- Measures (`healthCaps()`, at most one capability per measure, numbers only; from the test Homey's Airthings View, Aqara air monitor and heat pump, 2026-10-16): temperature (`measure_temperature`, without it `.room`/`.indoor`/`.inside`, never `.outdoor`/`.freezer`), humidity (the same), `measure_co2`, `measure_radon` (Bq/m³; pCi/L units × 37, also on realtime), `measure_pm25`, `measure_pm10`, VOC (`measure_voc`/`measure_VOC`/`measure_tvoc`, matched without case, only in ppb). Left out on purpose: CO (Sensor Alarms' job), AQI, PM1/PM0.1, TVOC in μg/m³ or as an index, NOx, string capabilities (`measure_radon_display`) and the `alarm_*` levels.
- The `devices` filter lists the exact ids (the web dashboards' `passesDeviceFilter` matches exactly); whether the phone's picker offers a device with only `measure_temperature.room` (the heat pump) or `measure_VOC` isn't known yet; the fallback is no filter, like Sensor Alarms.
- Settings: `renameTiles`, `colorStyle`, `layout` (`icons` default, `bars`; its hint lists the marks' order, as there's no legend), `greenWhenFine` (off: a fine mark is the muted grey, `.rh-green` makes it green), then the ranges (numbers, units in the titles, the highest temperature and humidity indented under the lowest): `tempMin` 18 (WHO's minimum) and `tempMax` 24 °C, `humidityMin` 30 and `humidityMax` 60 % (40 would call Nordic winters dry), `co2Max` 1000 ppm, `radonMax` 100 Bq/m³ (WHO), `pm25Max` 15 and `pm10Max` 45 μg/m³ (WHO 2021, 24 h), `vocMax` 1000 ppb. `roomHealthRange()` puts in the default for a missing, empty or non-numeric value and swaps a lowest above the highest.
- Score (`roomHealthScore()`): 0 inside the range, else `min(1, distance / margin)`: 3 °C, 10 points, half the limit for the concentrations (red at 1.5 ×). A room's is its worst measure's (ties: the first in `MEASURES` order).
- Rooms (`roomHealthRooms()`, front end, after Light Controls' `buildItems()`): by `zone.id` in the order picked (key `zone:<id>`; a sensor without a known zone, or a missing one, is a room of its own keyed by its id), each measure the average of the room's sensors with a reading. A device with nothing usable shows `–` and faint marks (`none`), a missing one `–` and `Unavailable` in muted text, without marks. A failed zones read gives the last zones (`readZones()`), so rooms don't split.
- A cell (`auto-fill` 96 px: 3 per row at 358 and 300 px, 4 at 420; 14 px padding) with hairlines between the rooms (variant B of five the user was shown, 2026-10-18, `temp/roomhealth-variants.html`: A separate tiles, C panels, D more space, E A at 24 px; B with E's 24 px value): each room an inset right and bottom line (`--rh-line`, Homey's light line colour), the grid 1 px past the clipping tile so the outer ones go, the last room without its right one. The value (`.rh-value`, 24 bold, the xlarge token; `°`/`%` attached, another unit after it at 14 muted) in its level's text colour when off (the 4.5:1 yellow/red pairs, not Default/Deep-dependent); `.rh-room` is a size container and the JS sets `--rh-em` (the number's width in ems: 0.58 a glyph, 0.27 a separator) and `--rh-unit-w`, so `min(24px, (100cqi − unit) / em)` shrinks a long value (`1,400 ppm`) to fit; the value line keeps a full-size height and baseline, so the names line up. The name (17 medium), then `.rh-marks`: one `.rh-mark` per column, the measures any room has in `MEASURES` order (`--rh-n`), so they line up across rooms; `data-level` `good`/`off`/`none`/`absent`. Bars: 6 px, up to 20 px wide, the track when `none`/`absent`. Icons (16 px, slots up to 24 px; `ICONS`: the kit's thermometer and drop, a cloud for CO₂, an atom for radon, small and large dots for PM2.5/PM10, a flask for VOC): hidden when `absent`, 40 % when `none`.
- A tap (`onTap()`, the Activity Dots/Flow Buttons copy) steps the value to the room's next reading from the one it shows (`.picking` tints the cell, the other marks at 35 %), back to the usual one past the last or after 6 s; not on a missing room, one with a single reading, or while renaming. The aria-label is `Room: State, readings…` (the state words `Good`, `Stuffy` …, `No readings`).
- `GET /state?deviceIds=` → `{id, name, zone: {id, name} | null, measures: {[measure]: {capabilityId, value}}}` or missing; realtime `roomhealth:state {deviceId, capabilityId, measure, value}` (in the measure's unit). Diagnostics' device list has each device's `health` capabilities. `dev/roomhealth-preview.html` (both layouts, green, Deep; `?open=zone:bed` steps that room once). Checked on the Homey through `/web/call` (2026-10-16); not yet seen in a browser or the phone apps, nor on a wall tablet.
- Colour style (`colorStyle`, Default/Deep; CLAUDE.md *Widget styling*, `kit.css`): after `renameTiles` (round 2, the user's ask: only the mark colours change). `--rh-arc-mix` (named for the rings): an off mark and a green one are their colour mixed 60 % into the track in Default (toned down), 100 % in Deep. The text colours don't change.
