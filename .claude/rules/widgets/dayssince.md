# Days Since widget (`dayssince`)

- Tap-to-reset counters for household rhythms (wk-062, the user's idea): plants watered 6 days ago, the vacuum filter 3 months. `lib/DaysSinceService.ts`.
- Layout (the user's design, 2026-10-18; it replaced Device Values-style tiles, 2 per row, which the user disliked): a list on Homey's own widget card (not transparent: `homey-widget`, like Charts; the mock's 16 px padding and 14 px radius are the card's), rows 16 px apart. A row (`.ds-row`, the tap target): the icon (20 px, Flow Buttons' glyphs as inline SVG, muted), the name and the time since (17/24 bold; the mock had 16), then a 6 px bar (`.ds-bar`, `--ds-fill`) and a 12/16 medium muted label (`.ds-left`). The rows are a `subgrid` of `.ds-list` (`20px 1fr auto`), so the label column is as wide as the widest label and every bar ends at the same x (a fixed 64 px column without subgrid). The time keeps its width; the name ellipsizes.
- The bar and label (`daysSinceProgress()`, exact time like the colours): the fill is the time since the reset ÷ `red` (else `amber`); the label `__time__ left` (`ceil` whole days to it, so at least 1) or, once past it, `+__time__` in the red text colour (`ceil` days over, from 1). Short units (`shortDay/Week/Month/Year`: `2 d`, `5 w`, `3 mo`; the user chose own strings over Intl's short or narrow units) through the same `amountOf()` as the time, so `units: days` gives `67 d left`. No interval: `.no-due` hides the bar and label (`visibility`). *Not started*: an empty bar, no label.
- Counters are shared by name (as Notes' notes): `counterKey()` (trimmed, lower-cased, ≤ 60; `__proto__` & co. refused; the widget's copy `daysSinceKey` is checked equal). The app setting `daysSince` `{[key]: {name, resetAt, amber, red, overdue, updatedAt}}`, at most 50 (the oldest `updatedAt` go; a widget showing one marks it seen, saved at most hourly), saved 2 s after a change; realtime `dayssince:state {key, name, resetAt, amber, red}`.
- Set up in the widget settings (the user's choice over the app settings page; 33 settings): `units` (`friendly`: days to 13, then weeks 2–8, months 2–11, years, each counted down; `days`: always days, `82 days`), then per counter N = 1…8 `counterN` (autocomplete `listCounters(q, {none, create})`: None, the known counters by name described by their days or *Not started*, then what's typed as *New counter*; the item id is the name), `iconN` (`listIcons(q, {none: true})`; unset is the clock, `none` no icon; the kit's `icons`), `amberN`/`redN` (days, default 5/7, 0 off; decimals work).
- The app learns the intervals from the widgets: `GET /state?counters=<JSON [{name, amber, red}]>` (`sync()`, at most 8) creates new counters and takes each one's intervals, so with two widgets that disagree the last loaded wins for the Flow cards (the red hint says so). The intervals come with a widget's first load only, its 5-minute refreshes send `[{name}]`: two widgets that disagreed took turns, and each turn fired the overdue trigger again; a counter in two slots takes the first's. Each widget colours by its own settings. Answers `{now, language, counters}` in order.
- Days and colours (the user's choice): the text counts **calendar days** in the screen's time (reset at 22:00 → `1 day` next morning; `Today` the same day); amber and red come **exactly N × 24 h** after the reset (`daysSinceLevel()`), so on day 7 it can say `7 days` while still amber until the hour of the reset. Words from `Intl.NumberFormat(…, {style: 'unit', unit: 'day'|'week'|'month'|'year', unitDisplay: 'long'})` in Homey's language with the screen's region (the Clock's order), so every language's plurals with no strings (`6 dage`, `2 дня`, `5 дней`); `6 d` without Intl units. Re-renders just after each minute; the Homey's `now` corrects the screen's clock (Timers').
- Colours: the bar blue (Homey's), amber yellow `#F5C518` (no token), red Homey's; the track and the Undo pill Variables' grey on the card (`color-mix` 8 % text). In Default (round 2 of Colours, 2026-10-18: the user found the coloured text too strong) the icon, time and labels stay in the text colours and the bar fills at 50 %; until then they were in the level's 4.5:1 text colours. No tint on the row. *Not started* is muted, regular.
- A tap (`onTap()`: Timers' and Locks' copy, which stops the tap at the Undo pill) resets at once to `Today` (`POST /reset {name}` → `{now, counter}`), with an **Undo** pill (Variables' grey pill, 30 px in the 24 px line, 14/20 bold, its tap area reaching past it) after the time for 5 s (`UNDO_MS`); row taps meanwhile do nothing, so a double tap doesn't lose the undo. Undo shows the old time at once and sends `POST /undo {name, at}` once the reset has answered (`at` is its `resetAt`). The app keeps each widget reset's previous `{resetAt, overdue}` for 60 s and only undoes while the counter's `resetAt` is still `at` (a Flow's reset since stays), else `{undone: false}`; the widget then shows the app's time. Failures shake the row with `dayssince.failed`/`undoFailed`.
- Flow cards (`app.ts` `registerDaysSinceFlows()`): actions `dayssince_reset` "Reset [[counter]]" and `dayssince_set` "Set [[counter]] to [[days]] days ago" (0–3650; the user's back-dating extra, for things done before the widget; a counter not yet shown is created without intervals), the trigger `dayssince_overdue` "[[counter]] became overdue" (tokens `counter`, `days` in Homey's timezone, `calendarDays()` on `localHour()`) and the condition `dayssince_is_overdue` "[[counter]] !{{is|isn't}} overdue" (the repo's first condition card); the last two also list *Any counter* (`{id: 'dayssince:any', any: true}`, matched by `any`). The trigger is filtered with `trigger(tokens, {key})` and a run listener (`matches()`). No due-soon cards (the user left them out).
- The trigger fires when the saved `overdue` flag turns true (`evaluate()`, on every change and a 1-minute tick: a timeout can't wait weeks): once per crossing, not again after a restart, at start for a counter that turned red while the app was stopped, again when a raised `red` is crossed later, at once when back-dating or a lowered `red` puts it past. Undoing a reset restores the flag, so it doesn't fire twice.
- Not a Smart Stack page (the user didn't pick it: not in `STACK_TYPES`, `TYPES` or `stack_show`; the stack test leaves it out), so the web dashboards reach its API through `WEB_APIS` in the root `api.ts`. No tile names (the counters are named in the settings). Diagnostics' `daysSince`: counts only.
- `dev/dayssince-preview.html` (a simulated app shared by the widgets, on drawn cards; `Broken` fails; `?lang=`), README screenshot `dayssince` (inline in `mock-dashboard.js`, on `.card`, Fish just reset with Undo), `MOUNT.dayssince` in `showcase-mount.js` (no showcase dashboard has one). `npm run test-dashboards -- dayssince` makes `Test · …` counters (they stay in the app setting). Checked on the Homey 2026-10-16: `/state` through `/web/call`, the Flow autocompletes, back-dating, the condition (also with Any counter), a Flow reset, and the trigger firing once (debug log). Not yet: a tap and Undo on a real dashboard or phone, Intl units in the phone apps' WebViews, and a Flow using the trigger.
- Colour style (`colorStyle`, Default/Deep; CLAUDE.md *Widget styling*, `kit.css`): after `units`. `--ds-blue` is the palette's. Default (toned down, the user's choice 2026-10-18): the icon, time and labels in the text colours, only the bar shows the level, filled at 50 % (`color-mix` into the track). Deep: an amber or red row stands on a solid block (`::before`, `inset: -6px -8px`, 10 px radius, under the row's `isolation`, so the subgrid is untouched): yellow with dark text, or the red shade with white; every bar fills at full strength, on a block in the text colour.
