# Media widget (`media`)

- One speaker as a card (a user's request: Homey's media widget had stale album art, greyed out on TV, had no mute, couldn't switch the source, and its volume lagged so the kids overshot). `lib/MediaService.ts` (no capability instances for the `speaker_next`/`prev` buttons; the re-read also refetches the icon, so a changed `iconOverride` shows). Not transparent: `homey-widget` with `.mw-frame`, the thermostat's dark frame.
- The speaker is the `device` autocomplete (`listDevices()`: anything with `speaker_playing`, `volume_set` or `volume_mute`).
- Switching speaker (`switchSpeakers`, on; a user's request, 2026-10-09): the name row (`.mw-who`: a 14 px device icon, the 12/16 name and a ⌄) opens the speaker list (`.mw-speakers`) from `GET /speakers` (`listSpeakers()`, by name). One condensed line per speaker (the user's request): the icon, the name and what it plays (left out when it only repeats the name: `mediaSource()`'s TV/line-in, `track · artist`, blue-ish while playing, else the zone); the current one tinted with a ✓. Re-read on each open (the last list meanwhile); closes on a pick, ✕, a tap beside the rows or 15 s. Unlike the ⋯ overlay it's `display: none` while closed, so a long list grows the widget.
  - The choice is per screen: `localStorage` `widgetkeeper.media.speaker.<widget instance id>` (cleared at the default; without storage it's always the default). A remembered speaker that comes back `missing` falls back to the default. The mount keeps the current `deviceId` (for `/set`, `/button`, `/art` and the realtime filter) and drops older `/state` answers (`loadSeq`). `setState` with another id clears the optimistic values, a pending volume burst, busy buttons and the overlay.
  - A switched-to speaker's `/state` is asked with `cards=1` and carries `cards` (its argument-free cards' `<card>` part, `cardSuffix()`). A card button whose `<card>` isn't there is hidden; `runButton()` matches cards by `<card>`, so `card:homey:device:<default>:cloud_play_home_theater` runs the switched-to speaker's own. Flows and the app's cards (`card:homey:app:…`, which `buttonCard()` doesn't match) always show.
- The device icon (`fetchDeviceIcon()`) is a mask (`.mw-mask`, `--mw-icon`) in the name row and, without album art (and TV/line-in), in the art box; without one, a drawn speaker glyph.
- Layout (condensed on request, 2026-10-09: no volume percentage, the buttons in an overlay): the 64 px album art (or a note, TV or plug glyph), the speaker's name, the track (17 bold) and artist · album, a progress line with `1:14 / 3:33` (`speaker_position`/`speaker_duration` are seconds, counted on locally while playing by a 1 s timer that stops while hidden; `opts.now` fixes it for the screenshot), and `⋯` top right. Then previous, play (blue), next, and mute on the right (red while muted); shuffle and repeat (blue when on, a `1` badge for one track) only with `showShuffle`. Then − bar +.
- `layout` (`compact` default; `large`, wk-046, a user's request for readability, `.mw-large`): the art box moves to the top of `.mw-main` as a full-width square, with larger text, buttons, pills and speaker rows (sized so shuffle and repeat fit 326 px; below 320 px, `@container mw`, the buttons go back to about compact). The same elements and logic; only the order and the `.mw-large` CSS differ.
- `showProgress` (on): off hides the progress line and stops its timer, so the row is as tall as the art.
- `volumeLayout` (`line` default; `buttons`): drops the volume line and puts − mute + right of the transport row (`.mw-volume-buttons`, sized to fit 268 px), with the level (to `maxVolume`) as a conic ring on mute (`.ring`, `--mw-x`). Shuffle and repeat then move into the overlay as pills.
- The overlay (`⋯`, shown only when it has something): `.mw-main` and `.mw-panel` share one grid cell in `.mw-card`, so the card is as tall as the taller of the two and opening it never changes the widget's height (keep the pills 36 px so the panel stays shorter). `.panel-open` hides the main view and shows the pills with a ✕. A tap beside the pills, ✕, `⋯` again or 8 s without a touch close it, and a button 600 ms after its check mark. `⋯` is tinted blue while a button in it is lit.
- TV and line-in (`mediaSource()`, also on `window`): the track `HDMI`/`TV`/`TV/HDMI`/`Line-In` (the Athom Sonos app reports TV as `HDMI`, 2026-10-09), else LocalAPI's `sonos_sound_input` while there's no track. The card says `TV` (`Muted` under it) with a TV glyph on a blue-tinted square instead of the stale art; previous, play and next step back (`.mw-external`); a button whose name has `TV`/`HDMI` (or `line`) is lit.
- Volume: `−`/`+` step by `volumeStep` (2/5/10 %, snapped to the step's grid) at once; a burst is sent 400 ms after the last tap as one absolute `volume_set`. Reports that don't match the value sent are ignored during the burst and 4 s after (older steps still arriving); a match or 10 s clears it. `maxVolume` (1–100 %) is the bar's right end and disables `+` there, and the service caps it again. Changing the volume unmutes. The bar is Light Controls'.
- Album art: `device.images`' `type: 'media'` entry (`mediaArt()`; the Athom Sonos app: `{id: 'albumart', imageObj: {url: '/api/image/<id>', lastUpdated}}`), loaded directly with a cache buster of `lastUpdated` plus the track and artist, swapped in once decoded; a failed direct load switches to `GET /art` (base64) for the session and reports it, as Cameras. After a track/artist/album change the service re-reads the device at 1.5 s and 6 s and sends `media:art {deviceId, art}` when it changed (drivers set the art after the track).
- Buttons (`button1`…`button4`, `buttonNIcon` (optional, Flow Buttons' `listIcons(query, {none: true})`; the kit's `icons`), `buttonNName`; `mediaButtonsFromSettings()`). `listButtons()` gives `None`, the speaker's own Flow action cards without arguments (`card:<card id>`, described `Speaker action`; not the `volume_*`/`speaker_*` cards the card already covers), then the argument-free cards of the speaker's app (wk-054, a user's request for the Sonos (LocalAPI) app's (`nl.xiut.sonoslocal`) *Scan the music library*; `card:homey:app:<appId>:<card>`, described `App action · <app name>`), then Flow Buttons' `listFlows()`. Only argument-free cards (the user's choice: widget settings can't show a card's argument fields) and only the speaker's own app (`deviceApp()`: the device's `ownerUri`, else `driverUri`, when it's `homey:app:…`), never Homey's own `homey:manager:*`/`homey:zone:*`/`homey:mood:*` cards (one reboots the Homey) or other apps'. **Neither Sonos app has a capability you can set for the source** (2026-10-09): both switch it only with device action cards (Athom: `cloud_play_home_theater` "Set source to TV", `cloud_play_line_in`; LocalAPI: `sonos_set_tv_input`).
  - `flow.getFlowCardActions()` (the app's token; read once a minute) lists every device's cards with `ownerUri: 'homey:device:<id>'` and `id: 'homey:device:<id>:<card>'`. `flow.runFlowCardAction({uri: ownerUri, id: <the full id>, args: {}})` runs one (the short id is `Not Found`; 2026-10-09). It needs `homey.flow`, so it goes through the personal API key; a key that may only start flows gets `keyScope`, and the widget says speaker actions need permission to manage flows. `runButton()` only runs an argument-free card of the widget's own speaker, or of an app that has a speaker (`speakerAppCard()`, which reads the devices: an app's card runs whichever speaker the widget is on); flows go through `FlowService.trigger()`.
  - App action cards (2026-10-16): `getFlowCardActions()` has them with `ownerUri: 'homey:app:<appId>'`, `id: 'homey:app:<appId>:<card>'` and `ownerName` (the app's name); a device's `ownerUri` and `driverUri` are that same `homey:app:<appId>` (the Athom Sonos speakers: `homey:app:com.sonos`, `driverId: 'homey:app:com.sonos:cloud'`). The test Homey's speakers are all Athom Sonos, which has no app cards, so the real LocalAPI card hasn't been run from the widget.
  - Testing the button autocomplete with `homey api raw`: `GET /api/manager/dashboards/appwidget/homey%3Aapp%3A<appId>%3Amedia/autocomplete?settingId=button1&query=&settings%5Bdevice%5D%5Bid%5D=<deviceId>` (settings as bracketed query keys; a JSON `settings=` is ignored).
- Endpoints: `GET /state?deviceId=[&cards=1]` (`{id, name, icon, caps: {<cap>: {value, setable, values?}}, art: {url, lastUpdated} | null, cards?}` or missing), `GET /speakers`, `POST /set {deviceId, capabilityId, value, maxVolume}` (only `speaker_playing`, `speaker_next`/`prev` (always `true`), `speaker_shuffle`, `speaker_repeat`, `volume_mute`, `volume_set`), `POST /button {deviceId, id}` (`{ok}` or `{ok: false, reason}`), `GET /art?deviceId=`, `POST /report {text}`. Realtime `media:state` and `media:art`. Refetch every 5 min and when the page becomes visible. Diagnostics' `media`: tracked speakers' capabilities, never track names.
