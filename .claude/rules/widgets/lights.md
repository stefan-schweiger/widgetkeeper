# Light Controls widget (`lights`)

- Compact light tiles, 2 per row, 88 px high, so 6 lights fit in the height of 3 native light cards (the user's aim); `lib/LightService.ts`; transparent, Quick Actions' tile style. `devices` filter: `class: light|socket`, `capabilities: onoff|dim` (plugs and switches set to be lights only have `onoff`; it's unknown whether the class filter matches a `virtualClass`, hence `socket`).
- A tile: the device icon and the colour chip on top, the name, then a brightness bar (28 px hit area, 6 px track, knob). No brightness text (dropped so the chip's tap area could grow). A lit tile takes the light's colour (`--lc-light`: `hueColor()` in colour mode, whiter as the saturation drops; else the cool → warm temperature colour), fading towards the bottom like the native card.
- **Brightness 0 = off** (the user's rule): the service turns `onoff` off and keeps `dim`; above 0 sets `dim`, then `onoff=true` if it was off. A light without `onoff` turns off with `dim=0` and back on to its last brightness above 0. A light without `dim` takes `/set {dim}` as on (above 0) or off; its tile (`.no-dim`) hides the bar and counts as full brightness while on. `barMin` (`0` default; `1` stops the bar at 1 %, `level()`, so the far left dims as low as it goes and stays on; a tap still toggles off).
- A tap on the tile toggles (`onTap()`, or Enter/Space: a focusable `role="group"` labelled with the name, not a button, since it holds the chip and the slider). The bar takes its own touches (`preventDefault`, `touch-action: none`): a drag updates live and sends once when let go, a tap sets the value where it lands. Android's dashboard takes a drag after ~100 ms (`touchcancel`), so there only a tap on the bar works.
- The chip (only with a settable `light_hue` or `light_temperature`): a 48 × 40 px button reaching into the tile's padding; a half-filled circle with `light_hue` (`HUE_GLYPH`), else a thermometer, drawn in `--lc-light` (dimmed while off). The user rejected a palette, a drop, RGB circles, a rainbow-ring colour well, a dot with an icon inside, and a tinted pill while its panel is open. Its taps go through `onTap()` with `stop`, so they never toggle the tile.
- The chip opens a swatch panel (`.lc-panel`) over the tile's grid row (absolute in `.lc-grid`, the tiles before it). The user chose swatches over a wheel or hue slider, and one chip for colour and whites (it replaced a bar temperature mode). Taps only, so it works on Android. 7 columns: 7 hues at full saturation; then 5 whites with `light_temperature`, else one white (saturation 0); ✕ last. A temperature-only light gets one row of whites. The current colour has a ring (from the optimistic values). A swatch sends `{hue, saturation}` or `{temperature}` and closes it; ✕, the chip, 8 s without a touch or the light going missing close it too.
- Setting a colour: `onoff=true` first if the light was off (a Hue bridge ignores colour changes while off, so it came on in its old colour; a user's report), then `light_mode` when present (`color`/`temperature`), then `light_hue` and `light_saturation`, or `light_temperature`.
- `palette` (`PALETTES`; an unknown id → `default`): the panel's 7 colours (`[hue°, saturation]`) and 5 whites. `default` (bright colours around the wheel, whites cool → warm), `warm` (red → orange → amber → pale amber, whites warm → cool: the user's "from red to cool white"), `dusk` (indigo → violet → rose → coral → amber at 0.6–0.85 saturation, warm whites only) and `moods`.
- Room's Moods (`palette: moods`, wk-031, a user's request): the panel shows only the Homey Moods of the tile's room (`zoneOf()`: a grouped tile's zone, else its light's `zone.id`; not parent zones) and ✕, no colours or whites (the user's choice, so more Moods fit). Pills in two rows that scroll sideways (`.lc-moods`; `.one-row` while they fit in one), each with a dot of the Mood's colours (`moodDot()`). A room without Moods keeps the colours and whites; a light without colour or temperature gets the chip (the hue glyph, labelled `Moods`) while its room has Moods. No current ring. A tap closes the panel and calls `opts.onMood(id)` (`POST /mood`); the lights' realtime shows the result. `{ok: false, reason}` shakes the tile with `KEY_PROBLEMS`' message, anything else `lights.moodFailed`.
  - homey-api's `ManagerMoods`: `getMoods()` → `{[id]: {id, name, preset, zone, devices: {[deviceId]: {state}}}}` needs `homey.mood.readonly` (the app's token has it); `setMood({id})` needs `homey.mood.set`, which it lacks (2026-10-15), so starting one goes through the personal API key. The state keys are capability ids (`onoff`, `dim`, `light_mode`, `light_hue`, `light_saturation`, `light_temperature`; Studio presets like `colorful` in `preset`; read with `MSYS_NO_PATHCONV=1 npx homey api raw -X GET --path /api/manager/moods/mood --json`). `LightService.getMoods(zoneIds)` shares one read for 60 s and gives `{id, name, zone, colors}` by name (`moodColors()`: per device that isn't turned off, `{hue, saturation}` in colour mode, else `{temperature}`, at most 4). `mount.js` asks `GET /moods?zones=` after each `/state` (only with `moods`; a failure keeps the last list). Verified on `Test · Light Controls` from headless Edge (2026-10-15): the Studio's three Moods with their dots, and a tap on Bloom set its lights through the stored key. Diagnostics' `moods`: counts per zone, the starts and the last error.
- `groupByZone` (off): the selected lights in one zone share a tile (`buildItems()`; id `zone:<zoneId>`, the zone's name, its first light's icon), placed where its first light is; a zone with one selected light, a light without a known zone and a missing light keep their own tile. Only the selected lights count. On while any light is; the bar shows the average brightness of those on. A tap turns all off when any is on, else all on; the bar sets every light; a colour goes to the lights with `light_hue`, a white to those with `light_temperature` and, in a room, as saturation 0 to colour lights without it. One `/set` per light, in parallel.
- Several colours (the user's request, 2026-10-15): `colorsOf()` is a tile's lit lights' colours (`colorOf()`, the shown values), without repeats, at most 4; a Homey group counts its lit lights instead of itself. With more than one, `.multi` and `setBlend()` blend them into the tile (`--lc-tint-bg`), the bar (`--lc-fill-bg`) and the chip (`--lc-pie`, `PIE_GLYPH`); `--lc-light` stays the first. Nothing on: the lead light's colour (`lead()`).
- Homey group devices (`driverId: 'homey:virtualdrivergroup:driver'`, the lights in `settings.deviceIds`, 2026-10-15; `GROUP_DRIVER`, `groupMembers()`): the group's own `light_hue` is only the last colour set on the group, so after a Mood it was wrong. `/state` gives a group `groupLights: [{id, caps}]` (tracked by the same `DeviceTracker`, so they send `lights:state`; one level; groups, missing devices and devices without `dim`/`onoff` left out; the members are in the `signature`). A `/state` reads each device once; a member that isn't a light (`NotALight`) or is a group isn't read again for 10 min (`MEMBER_SKIP_MS`). `mount.js` passes those ids' realtime too (`watched`), `pushChange()` updates them inside their group (`withGroup()`), and `hope()` on a group hopes its lights. Verified on `Test · Light Controls` after the Studio's Colorful Mood.
- `GET /state` entries: `{id, name, icon, zone: {id, name} | null, caps: {onoff?, dim?, light_*?: {value, setable}}, groupLights?}` (a device needs `dim` or `onoff`). `POST /set {deviceId, dim | onoff | temperature | hue + saturation}` (fractions 0–1), `GET /moods?zones=a,b`, `POST /mood {id}` (`keyResult()`). Realtime `lights:state`.
- `dev/lights-preview.html?open=<id>` opens a tile's panel (`d`, `h`, `a`, `zone:z1`); it has a grouped widget, a Homey group tile and three `moods` widgets (`Movie night` fails with `noKey`). `npm run test-dashboards -- lights` has a `moods` widget grouped by room.
