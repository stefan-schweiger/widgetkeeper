# Curtains widget (`curtains`)

- Curtains and blinds (a user's request: Athom dropped the tap-to-toggle for curtains and kept only a slider) as Light Controls' tiles, 2 per row, 104 px: the icon and the action pill on top, the name (14/20 bold), the state (12/16 muted: `Open`, `Closed`, `42% open`, `Opening…`, `Closing…`, `Stopped`) and Light Controls' position bar (Android only gets the tap). An open (any part) or moving tile is tinted blue. `lib/CurtainService.ts`; transparent. The user picked tiles over rows with Open/Stop/Close buttons.
- Capabilities (`CURTAIN_CAPS`; a device needs one, the picker filters on them): `windowcoverings_set` (the position: 0 closed, 1 open, Homey's standard; a user's SwitchBot curtains report it reversed, hence `invert`), `windowcoverings_state` (`up` opens, `down` closes, `idle` stops), `windowcoverings_closed` and `windowcoverings_tilt_set`. Without a settable position the bar is hidden (`.no-position`).
- Blinds that open at 50 % (`openAt`, after `invert`): a SwitchBot Blind Tilt (wk-036) and vertical slatted blinds on Zemismart ZM85EL-1Z chain motors (wk-026), where 0 and 1 are both closed and 0.5 is open. A device without `windowcoverings_set` uses its tilt as the position (`posCap()`; the service's `{position}` goes to `windowcoverings_set`, else the tilt; `{action}` never to the tilt). The SwitchBot app's hub driver has only the tilt, the BLE one also `windowcoverings_closed`; both read 0 = slats down, 0.5 = open, 1 = slats up, and the hub one reports `null` while it turns (AdyRock/com.switchbot's source, 2026-10-15). Values: `end` (Homey's standard; `invert` applies), `middleTilt` (the default: 50 % only for tilt-only blinds) and `middle` (every blind). A middle-open blind's open-ness is `1 − |2p − 1|` (`toOpen()`); back (`fromOpen()`) stays on the side of the last position (`lastSide`, the lower when unknown): Open sends 0.5, Close the nearer end. Its motor's `up`/`down` is ignored for the direction (`motorSaysDirection()`: going up past 0.5 closes it). A blind with both a position and a tilt keeps `windowcoverings_set` (no tilt control). Not yet tried on a real one.
- A tap does what the pill says (`curtainAction()`, also on `window`; the user's concern was that a half-open curtain doesn't say which way it'll go): moving → the other way (the user chose that over a stop; the motor's `up`/`down` is hoped over for 10 s); open within 5 % (`END`: some curtains stop at 1 % and 99 %) → `Close`; closed → `Open`; in between, `between`: `nearer` (default; above half closes) or `reverse` (opposite the last move, like a one-button remote; nearer while unknown). Without a position: `windowcoverings_closed`, else the reverse of the last move, else `Open`. The pill is 12/16 medium with filled triangles in the move's direction (`actionGlyph()`: ◀ ▶ apart to open, ▶ ◀ together to close; ▲/▼ for blinds). The user rejected stroked arrows, drawings of the curtain as it will be, and an options page of other glyphs.
- Moving: the motor's `up`/`down`, or `pending` after a tap or a bar change (the target and direction until the position is within 5 %, the motor reports `idle`, `windowcoverings_closed` reports, or 60 s); a thin mark on the bar shows the target (`.heading`, `--ct-target`). A realtime report always replaces the optimistic value (a curtain reports where it is on its way). The last direction (`lastDir`) comes from taps, the motor and position changes, per widget page.
- `invert` ("Position 100% means closed", off, for the whole widget): the widget works in open-ness, and `toOpen()`/`fromOpen()` flip the position both ways, so a tap on a curtain with a settable position sends `{position}`, not `{action}`; the motor's `up`/`down` isn't flipped (unknown whether inverted drivers flip it).
- The service moves `{action}` through the position when there is one, else the motor, else `windowcoverings_closed`. No stop. Not yet tried on a real curtain.
- The icon is drawn: a rod with two drapes that part with the position (half open when unknown), or for blinds and sunshades (`kind`, `virtualClass || class`) a rising panel.
- `showState` (on): off adds `.ct-no-state` (88 px tiles). `groupByZone` as Light Controls': the average position; a tap moves all the same way, the bar sets every one with a position.
- `GET /state` entries: `{id, name, kind, zone, caps: {windowcoverings_*: {value, setable}}}`. `POST /set {deviceId, position}` (the device's own scale) or `{deviceId, action: open|close}`. Realtime `curtains:state`.
- Colour style (`colorStyle`, Muted/Vivid; CLAUDE.md *Widget styling*, `kit.css`): last, with `color` (an open or moving tile, blue by default; `--ct-accent` is `--wk-c`). Vivid: the open tile is the shade, solid, with `--wk-on` text, fill and a 22 % track.
