Widgetkeeper adds dashboard widgets that feel like part of Homey. Each one is designed to sit naturally alongside Homey's built-in widgets, with the same look and feel.

The widgets cover energy, with live power use, hourly electricity prices and a compact price badge; climate, with thermostat presets, a 36-hour weather forecast, heatmaps and small charts of any sensor value; security, with sensor alarms, motion and door sensors at a glance, cameras with live view, and locks and doors; and everyday control, with quick actions, light controls with colours, curtains with one-tap open and close, a speaker card with mute, a volume limit and source buttons, device values, flow variables, flow buttons and kitchen timers. Many of them are compact tiles, so more fits on a dashboard. A Smart Stack shows several of them in one spot, one at a time, and brings forward the one that matters, such as the cameras when someone is at the door.
